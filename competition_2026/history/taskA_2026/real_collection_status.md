images, images with more than one predicted box fall from 63 to 33 and the
maximum boxes per image falls from 7 to 4. Duplicate boxes remain concentrated
in real_v5 (20 images) and in the new-view Cheez-it and meat-can classes.

Decision: do not promote this checkpoint to the live camera service and do not
replace the existing production/reference weights. The next training attempt
must improve both real_v5 and real_v9 without a regression on either, then
repeat this grouped evaluation and the multi-box check. Simple confidence or
NMS sweeps may reduce duplicates, but they cannot repair the real_v5 box-scale
regression.

A post-hoc confidence sweep confirmed that limitation. Raising confidence from
0.25 to 0.35 leaves real_v9 unchanged at 72.2% pass and 100% recall, while
real_v5 recall falls further from 37.5% to 23.3%. The sweep can suppress
duplicate boxes and false positives, but it cannot recover the old-view
detections or improve their box accuracy.
